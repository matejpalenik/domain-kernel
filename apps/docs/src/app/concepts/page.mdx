# Understand the modelling lifecycle

Domain Kernel's design separates evidence, proposed meaning, human decisions and
published contracts. This separation lets you inspect where an error occurred and
preserve the reasons behind the model your application consumes.

## Evidence is not an accepted definition

Parsing creates addressable text, tables and other evidence. Discovery proposes
concepts and relationships from that evidence. Per-run instructions can focus
discovery on a use case, while the SDK's output contract stays fixed. A proposal
still needs review before becoming part of the accepted model.

## Review precedes publication

Manual authoring and discovery feed change plans and review cases. You inspect a
decision's impact before submitting it. Publication prepares a validated snapshot
and releases an immutable bundle, retaining source evidence and attributed history.

These lifecycle operations remain planned. The [notebook](/tour) makes their
intended behaviour inspectable through simulated results.

## Ontology reuse preserves distinctions

A local concept can relate to a reference term without having exactly the same
meaning. A recruitment AI system and a high-risk recruitment AI system, for
example, must not become interchangeable simply because their names are similar.
The model should retain the mapping direction, its evidence and the review decision.
